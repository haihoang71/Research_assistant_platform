# Project goals

## Primary goal

Create a research assistant that reduces the time needed to produce a first literature-review draft while preserving verifiable citations and human ownership of the final result.

## Quality goals

- Evidence-backed claims.
- Reproducible retrieval and evaluation.
- Explicit uncertainty.
- Clear audit trail for research runs.
- Architecture that can later support asynchronous workers and external vector stores.

## Non-goals for the foundation phase

- No production deployment.
- No autonomous publication of research reports.
- No unrestricted code execution.
- No support for every academic domain from day one.
